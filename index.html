About me

Hi, I'm Moa — a frontend developer based in Sweden with a background in beauty, design and customer experience.

Before moving into tech I worked as a skin therapist, trainer and clinic manager, where I learned to understand people, solve problems quickly and turn ideas into real experiences. Discovering frontend development felt like the perfect way to combine my love for creativity, structure and problem-solving.

Today I focus on building responsive and user-friendly websites while continuously expanding my skills as a developer.
